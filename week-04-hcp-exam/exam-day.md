# Exam day

**Register:** [HashiCorp certifications](https://developer.hashicorp.com/certifications/infrastructure-automation)  
**Cost:** $70.50 USD + tax. No free retake.  
**Proctor:** Certiverse. Webcam, photo ID, empty private room, closed notes.

## Timing

1 hour. Flag and move. Multi-answer items need **every** correct option.

Read the verb: explain, differentiate, describe when to use.

No live `terraform apply`. Selected response only.

## Last 30 minutes before you sit

1. Week 4 notes "Exam traps"
2. FLASHCARDS.md, missed cards only
3. Official sample questions (wording match)

Do not cram AWS APIs.

## After

Immediate pass/fail. Objective-level feedback usually within 48 hours. Badge lasts 2 years. Recertify on 004 starting 6 months before expiry, or take the Advanced exam.
